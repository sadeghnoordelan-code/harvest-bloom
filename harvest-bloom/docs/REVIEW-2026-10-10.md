# Harvest Bloom review — 2026-10-10

Reviewed the React components, hooks, catalogue, cart, payment seam, styles, public assets, build configuration and local asset tooling. Reproduced the mobile navigation overlay on https://harvest-bloom.vercel.app/ and confirmed all six deployed product images decode at 729 × 728 pixels. The reported image issue was layout, rather than missing uploads.

## Corrections

- Respect the hidden mobile-navigation attribute; add Escape handling and navigation relationships.
- Bound the focused product image by the available stage dimensions. Use a horizontal arc for stacked layouts and keep arrows separate from product selectors on small screens.
- Disable collection pinning on short screens so purchasing controls remain reachable; retain vertical page scrolling for touch.
- Delay pointer capture until an actual drag, preserving product taps; recover after cancelled gestures and breakpoint changes.
- Load the six small product assets eagerly so products do not appear blank when selected from off screen.
- Initialise saved cart data before persistence; discard invalid and duplicate product identifiers, including inherited object-property names.
- Cancel abandoned video downloads, provide a bounded download timeout, and shorten the hero on video failure.
- Render reduced-motion atmospheric decoration without a continuous animation loop; tolerate an unavailable canvas context.
- Keep contact text within narrow screens and section anchors below the fixed header.
- Remove developer implementation instructions and the false claim that an unpaid order has been submitted from the checkout notice.
- Correct local asset-tool path checks to reject sibling paths that merely share the permitted directory prefix. These routes remain disabled in production and ordinary development.
- Add Vercel configuration for both repository-root and harvest-bloom-root project settings.

## Mouse-wheel navigation and detail alignment

Scroll-derived targets now stop at whole product positions so the selected pack settles opposite its own detail panel. On short and stacked layouts, a wheel listener over the collection advances products without depending on sticky pinning; ordinary page scrolling resumes at either end. Control-wheel zoom and horizontal gestures remain available. The animation uses elapsed time across slower frames to avoid excessive settling delay. The panel exposes its catalogue identifier to verify synchronization with the focused image.

Run npm run test:wheel with the preview running to check real forward/reverse mouse-wheel input, every catalogue description and price, vertical alignment on wide layouts and page-scroll release at the last product. The cases cover Chromium and WebKit at 1440×900, 1440×640, 1024×768 and 390×844. The established ring geometry is unchanged.

## Right-side cycle and separated product images

The previous radius containment changed the radius without updating angular spacing, so narrower columns could make neighbouring packs overlap. Placing the circle centre at the stage edge also brought the focus too close to the information panel. The latest user correction requires the cycle to occupy the right side, including narrow screens.

The circle centre now continues beyond the stage's right edge; the focus sits at 60–65% of the right column. Pack size follows both column width and available height. Angular separation is calculated from that same radius and leaves a chord of at least 1.55 pack widths. The loop clips its contents to its own column, controls use separate side lanes, and the ribbon fades at the viewport boundaries. The small-screen layout also uses left-side details and a right-side ring, preserving weight and price access. This supersedes the earlier horizontal mobile arc and the previous fixed reference-radius checks.

Unit checks cover right-side focus, pack containment and neighbour spacing. The wheel browser checks now verify actual visible image rectangles do not overlap, every visible pack stays beyond the information panel, the focus is in the rightmost 30% of the page, and the panel and focus share a vertical centre at every tested width. The existing checks for all six products, descriptions, prices, weight editing, stable details scrolling and boundary release remain.
